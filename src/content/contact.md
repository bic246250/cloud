# Contact — Bump Compass

We read every message. We cannot provide medical advice, and we will not try.

## What we can help with

- **Errors** — a tool giving a wrong result, a broken calculation, a page that does not load
- **Sources** — a citation that is outdated, a claim that needs a better authority, a disagreement between sources we should surface
- **Suggestions** — a tool you wish existed, a guide you wish we had written

## What we cannot help with

- **Medical questions** — if you are pregnant, postpartum, or concerned about your baby, please contact your own clinician. We are not a clinic and we do not provide medical advice.
- **Product complaints** — if you have an issue with a product you purchased, contact the retailer or manufacturer directly.
